01 — WHY WE EXISTSTORY / MISSION / VISION

THE PROBLEM WITH PLAUSIBLE

A convincing answer isn't always a fair one.

AI systems are becoming part of decisions that affect real lives. Yet a polished answer can hide missing context, unsupported claims, and patterns that overlook underserved communities.

Traditional benchmarks can miss real-world questions. Fact-checking may identify inaccuracies without revealing systematic bias. Human review matters, but is difficult to scale. Veritas begins with a different question: what does the evidence actually show?

01 / MISSION

Make AI accountable.

Give teams a practical way to identify potential bias in AI outputs through checks against authoritative information.

02 / VISION

Make trust visible.

Imagine AI decisions whose underlying evidence can be examined, compared, and challenged—not simply taken on faith.

03 / VALUES

Evidence. Equity. Clarity.

We value rigorous verification, sensitivity to underrepresented communities, and transparent findings people can act on.
